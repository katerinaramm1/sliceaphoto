Slice a Photo

Turn one image into 2–20 numbered slices, left to right or top to bottom.

Look for quiet gaps near each cut to reduce splits through text. With Slice only, smart cuts can produce slightly different file dimensions.

Slice only exports the image content at its original scale. Fixed-size presets add padding as needed to match the selected dimensions. Original scale gives all slices a common canvas size.

Your image is processed on your device.
